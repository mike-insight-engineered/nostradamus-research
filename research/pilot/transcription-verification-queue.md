## After verification

1. The transcriber corrects the draft. The checker compares independently and records results on the sheet.
2. Update the passage record (corrected text, new hash) and append a `transcription_checked` review event naming
   the checker, plus `locator` and `facsimile_reference` taken from the library's own viewer.
3. Only then set `verification_status: transcription_verified` and import with
   `nr passages import <file> --by "<Name>"`. The source must first reach `provenance_checked` with
   `catalogue_record` evidence by a named person (`nr sources promote`).
