## Translation evaluation

- Freitag, Markus, et al. "Experts, Errors, and Context: A Large-Scale Study of Human Evaluation for Machine Translation." *Transactions of the ACL* 9 (2021).
- Popović, Maja. "chrF: character n-gram F-score for automatic MT evaluation." *Proceedings of the Tenth Workshop on Statistical Machine Translation* (2015).
- Popović, Maja. "chrF++: words helping character n-grams." *Proceedings of the Second Conference on Machine Translation* (2017).
