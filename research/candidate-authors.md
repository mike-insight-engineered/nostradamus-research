corresponding registry record reaches `provenance_checked`. Nostradamus is deliberately excluded
from M1.

## Mixed / difficult (pilot 5 / full 10)

- Rabelais: latinising and macaronic passages (e.g. the "écolier limousin" episode in *Pantagruel*).
- Texts mixing French with Latin quotation (sermons, legal or medical writing): to identify.
- Dense abbreviation in early printed books: to identify from facsimiles.
- Occitan/Gascon/Provençal: e.g. Pey de Garros (Gascon) and Louis Bellaud de la Bellaudière
  (Provençal). Relevance to the Provençal context of Nostradamus; translation references are harder
  to find. *(Not registered; details to verify.)*

## Selection cautions

- Famous passages are more likely to appear in pretraining data together with translations. Record
  this, and prefer less-reproduced passages.
- Avoid passages whose meaning depends on extensive context outside the excerpt.
