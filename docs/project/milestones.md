# Milestones

| Milestone | Goal | Gate | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1 — Historical Translation Calibration Pilot** | Establish whether AI pipelines translate period French/Latin reliably (QC1/QC2) | Feasibility gate after the 20-passage pilot; completion gate after 80 passages (CAL-14) | Software foundation ready for review; research not started |
| M2 — Nostradamus witnesses and translation | Authenticate early editions; diplomatic transcriptions; calibrated translations of selected quatrains | Requires an M1 "continue" decision | Not started |
| M3 — Interpretation evidence (QC3) | Structured, evidence-graded assessment of competing interpretations, incl. claimed wordplay/anagrams | Requires M2 | Not started |

## M1 gates

1. **Readiness (now):** `reports/milestone-1-readiness.md`. The software criteria pass; research
   criteria are failed or not evaluated. Recommendation: **DEFER** the go/no-go decision until the
   pilot data exists.
2. **Feasibility gate** (after CAL-10): pilot quotas met, references independent, blinded review
   complete with agreement reported. Decide whether to expand to 80 passages, remediate the method,
   or stop.
3. **Completion gate** (CAL-14): A–D benchmarked; pre-registered test run reviewed; calibration
   report with intervals. Decide **continue** (to M2), **remediate**, or **defer**.

Decisions are made and signed by the project lead and recorded in `docs/decisions/`.
